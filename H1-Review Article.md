b) Review the Review

 Key Research Areas:
   The classification of psychological factors (PFs) used in Internet-based social engineering attacks, such as trust, fear, authority and urgency.
   Analysis of the most common types of digital attacks: phishing, spear-phishing, baiting, pretexting, in email, web and social media.
   Assessment of current technical detection techniques and behavioral/human-centric defense techniques.

 Concepts that are generally agreed upon.
   We will always find weak attitudes and human behaviours as weak spots for cybersecurity.
   Technical safeguards (firewalls, spam filters) are not enough if the end-users can be successfully manipulated to defeat the security.

 Open Questions & Future Challenges:
   Designing adaptive and real-time security warnings that don't create user habituation and cognitive fatigue.
   Balance defensive structures with the psychological approaches used by social engineering attacks.

 My Thought / Question:
   Is it possible to create an AI-powered security awareness solution that can accurately forecast how susceptible a particular user is to security threats based on their current actions, without violating privacy?
